# SELVOF 14-Day Home Reset Kit — Quick-Start Guide

Welcome to the **SELVOF 14-Day Home Reset Kit** (Edition No. 04).

This system is engineered specifically for busy individuals and households seeking a mindful, realistic, and lasting transformation of their living spaces. Rather than exhausting weekend marathons, this workbook guides you through manageable **30 to 60-minute daily micro-interventions**.

## 2. The 3 Golden Rules of the Reset

Before touching a single drawer or shelf:

1. **Rule 1: Set a Strict Timer**  
   Commit to your allotted daily duration (30–60 minutes). When your timer chimes, stop immediately. Consistency beats intensity.
2. **Rule 2: Never Pull Out More Than You Can Put Away**  
   Work one drawer, one shelf, or one surface at a time. This prevents mid-session clutter panic and ensures you always finish on time.
3. **Rule 3: Respect Decision Limits & Use the 30-Day Pause Box**  
   If you hesitate over an item for more than 30 seconds, do not debate it. Place it in the Pause Box, date it 30 days ahead, and let time clarify your decision.

## 5. Printing Recommendations

- **Paper Choice**: For an authentic SELVOF luxury feel, print on warm white or natural ivory text weight paper (100–120 gsm / 28–32 lb text).
- **Scale Setting**: In your printer dialog, select **"Actual Size"** or **"Scale: 100%"** (do not select "Fit to Printable Area" as margins are pre-calculated).
- **Binding**: Ideal for brass clipboards, leather portfolios, coil binding, or hole-punching for linen binders.
