ETERNAL MOTION

Always in motion. Further ahead.

ETERNAL MOTION explores movement, direction and the space between where you are and where you're going.

Built on a clean white oversized silhouette, the piece features a highly detailed back graphic combining orbital forms, celestial elements, technical markings and the SEVOLF insignia. A smaller matching emblem sits on the left chest, creating a visual connection between the front and back.

The result is a statement piece that feels technical without losing its restraint.

BETWEEN DISTANCES

Somewhere between where you are and where you're going, movement becomes its own direction.

BETWEEN DISTANCES is a heavyweight oversized sweatshirt built around the idea of distance, orientation and forward motion.

A celestial composition brings together orbital geometry, mapped terrain, planetary forms and architectural lines — creating a visual language that sits somewhere between a map and an imagined universe.

The front graphic is finished in restrained ivory and antique-gold tones against deep black, giving the piece a distinctive contemporary character without overwhelming the silhouette.

Designed as part of the SEVOLF collection for those who move beyond familiar coordinates.
